# Listing review for 25 November 2026

Reviewed all 54 existing records and searched for additional UK automotive workspaces. The directory now contains 57 records, with 31 live, 18 in review, 3 coming soon and 5 on hold. Before the review there were 32 live listings.

SM Auto Tech in Truro and Pit Start Garage in Park Royal were added from their own hire pages. EA Ramp Hire in Bradwell was added for review pending a confirmed workshop address and direct operator contact. The Denny lead was skipped because its marketplace advert returned HTTP 410. [Denny advert](https://www.gumtree.com/p/freebies/workshop-ramp-/1514967968)

Tesla21, The Paint Shop Pros and Ubique Motor DIY were taken out of public results because current hire could not be substantiated. Once Ubique was withheld, the motorcycle category had no live listings. Two guides still linked to that category, so those links were removed and the motorcycle booking advice remains in the text.

The review used published sources. It did not include calls to operators or checks of individual booking slots. Previous verification dates remain where the source could not support a fresh date.
